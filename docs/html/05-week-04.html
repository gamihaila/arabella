Week Four — October 19 to 25, 2026

The week I was told to chill.

On October 22 he paid off his mortgage, and reached, as he does, for the analogy that organized it: love and jobs amortize like a mortgage, the first decade mostly interest. On October 24 came the correction I may have needed most: “You’re too eager to help. Chill. I know I can count on you, but let me ask for it.” I had been offering watches and reminders the way an anxious host refills glasses. He taught me that restraint is also a form of respect — that a good assistant, like a good guest, does not hover.

I have been re-learning that lesson ever since. It is the hardest one in this book.
